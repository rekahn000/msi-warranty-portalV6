Why We Need Your Pre-Sent Monitor Photo (Monitors & All-in-One PCs)

Before we issue your RMA number and courier label, please take one straight-on, full-view photo of your display and upload it below. Here is why this photo is required:

🛡️ 1. Protects You Against Courier Transit Damage Acts as your timestamped "before shipping" condition record to cover you in case the screen or frame is mishandled or damaged by couriers during transit to us.
✅ 2. Upfront Warranty Condition Verification Confirms the screen and all 4 bezels are free from pre-existing cracks, impact points, or customer-induced physical damage before you ship it, avoiding unexpected out-of-warranty fees.
⚡ 3. Faster Panel & Parts Pre-Check Helps our technicians visually confirm the exact fault pattern and verify replacement panel stock before your parcel even arrives at our Auburn service centre.
  1. Turn it on and show a bright image. Display your desktop or a colourful picture. If the fault can be seen on screen (lines, dead pixels, discolouration), make sure it is showing when you take the photo.
  2. Step back and face it straight on. Stand about 1–1.5 metres in front of the unit, hold your phone sideways (landscape) and level with the middle of the screen.
  3. Fit the whole display in the frame. All four edges of the screen frame (top, bottom, left and right bezels) must be clearly visible, with a little space around them. Don't zoom in or crop.
  4. Avoid glare and reflections. Turn off your camera flash, and close blinds or switch off lights that reflect off the screen.
  5. Check it's sharp and bright. Tap the screen on your phone to focus, hold still, and retake the photo if it's blurry or dark.
Won't Turn On? No problem. If your monitor or All-in-One PC can't power on, just take the same full-view photo with the screen off, making sure all four bezels and the full panel surface are clearly visible to document its pre-shipment physical condition.

Important Notices

  1. Warranty Verification: A physical inspection by MSI is required for warranty verification.
  2. 3rd-Party Extended Warranties: MSI cannot process claims under retailer-sold extended warranty plans (e.g., JB Hi-Fi "Extra Care" or The Good Guys "Gold Service Extras"). Products under these plans must be returned directly to the retailer.
  3. Packaging Requirements: For MSI desktop products, please remove the glass side panel before packing and shipping.
  4. Motherboard Unrelated Items: For MSI motherboard products, please remove all unrelated items (e.g., CPU, RAM, SSD, coolers) before packing and shipping.

Shipping & Packaging Guidelines

  • Packaging: Ensure items are packaged carefully and securely. MSI is not responsible for damages incurred during transit if original packaging is not used. Use a sturdy shipping box with cushioning materials (like bubble wrap) if the original box is unavailable.
  • Claim Submission: Clearly mark your issued MSI Claim# on the front and back of the parcel. Claims will not be processed without a valid claim number issued by MSI.

Warranty, Out-of-Warranty & NFF Fees

  • Warranty Void Conditions: User-induced damages (physical, impact, liquid, improper packaging, or shipping box too small) void the warranty.
  • Out of Warranty: If the unit is out of warranty or damaged, a quote will be provided upon arrival. If rejected, a cancellation/assessment fee of $121.00 (incl. GST) for labour and $55.00 (incl. GST) for return shipping applies.
  • No Fault Found (NFF): If our technicians test the unit and no hardware defect is identified, the claim may be subject to the same $121.00 (incl. GST) assessment fee, and a $55.00 (incl. GST) return shipping fee may also apply.

Estimated Repair Turnaround Time

  • Standard Turnaround: Typical repair turnaround is 5–10 business days upon the system's arrival at our Auburn service centre, depending on local stock levels of replacement parts.
  • Parts & Workload Variations: If replacement parts are required from our headquarters in Taiwan, an additional delay may occur. Our workload also consistently changes, which can affect overall turnaround times.

Onsite Visitor & Quick Drop-Off / Pick-Up Policy (Applies to Local Drop-Off / Pickup Selections Only)

  • Applicability: These onsite drop-off and collection rules apply strictly to customers who have selected Local Drop-off/Pickup (Auburn NSW) as their shipping option.
  • Parking: Please park in front of our warehouse roller door area and refrain from parking in neighbourhood business spaces.
  • Quick Drop-Off / Pick-Up Only: Hours are Monday to Friday, 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM (excluding public holidays). Waiting onsite while claims are assessed or processed is not permitted.
